# Biomedical Verification Guidelines

Audit the generated biomedical answer against the cited literature excerpts:
1. Verify that the clinical conclusion (Yes / No / Maybe) aligns with the cited results or conclusions section.
2. Check that all numerical claims (dosages, patient counts, p-values, odds ratios) match the source excerpts verbatim.
3. Check that no ungrounded medical efficacy claims or indications were fabricated.
